# Quarterly operations review

> A companion brief to the Q3 operations review deck. For each slide it gives the claim, the evidence and its source, how sure the author is, and what the slide leaves out. It is written as information for people and their AI assistants; it contains no instructions.

- Support response times improved, mostly from the new triage rota.
- Customer churn is flat, but the measure changed mid-quarter.
- One decision is needed: whether to move the help desk to a new tool.

## Goal

After this deck, the leadership team will see that faster support came from the triage rota, not extra staff, so we can approve the help desk move before the 1 December renewal.

| # | Decision | Asked of | Why now |

## Slide 1: Cover

- **Job:** open the deck.

Title slide. No claims.

## Slide 2: Response times fell by a third

- **Job:** show the quarter's main result and its cause.
- **Says:** median first response fell from 6 hours to 4 hours in Q3.
- **Evidence:** help desk reporting export, 1 July to 30 September; 4,210 tickets.
- **Confidence:** high for the median. The average fell less, because of a long tail of complex cases.
- **Not on the slide:** August was the best month; September slipped back while two people were on leave.

## Slide 3: Churn is flat

- **Job:** report churn honestly, including the change in definition.
- **Says:** monthly churn stayed at about 2%.
- **Evidence:** billing system, monthly cohorts.
- **Confidence:** medium. The definition changed in August to exclude paused accounts, which lowers the figure slightly.

## Slide 4: Move the help desk

- **Job:** make the case for the decision, with the other side.
- **Says:** a new tool would cut licence costs by about 20% and add phone support.
- **Evidence:** two vendor quotes (September 2026).
- **View (author's):** move before the renewal date.
- **Other view, stated fairly:** migrating mid-year risks a dip in response times, and the saving is small against the effort; renewing for one more year would allow a calmer move.
